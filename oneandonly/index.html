A certified technician comes to your home the same day, diagnoses the problem, and gives you an exact flat-rate quote — all for $39. If you approve the repair, the $39 is applied toward the work. No hidden fees, no pressure.

How fast can you actually get here?+

We offer same-day service in every metro we cover, seven days a week. When you call, the dispatcher gives you a real arrival window for today — not "sometime this week."

How much does a garage door spring replacement cost?+

Most spring replacements fall within a flat-rate range that depends on your door's size and spring type. You'll get an exact price before any work begins — the quote you approve is the price you pay.

Do you charge extra for nights, weekends, or holidays?+

No. Our 24/7 availability is real availability — the rate is the same whenever you call.

Can I replace a garage door spring myself?+

Please don't. Garage door springs are under extreme tension and cause thousands of serious injuries every year. It's the one repair every manufacturer says to leave to trained technicians with proper tools.

Do you work on my door and opener brand?+

Yes — we repair and install all major brands including LiftMaster, Genie, Chamberlain, Raynor, Wayne Dalton, Clopay and Amarr, plus older and off-brand systems.
