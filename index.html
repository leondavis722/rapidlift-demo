Questions

Before You Call

How fast can you actually get here?+

Our average arrival time is about 60 minutes for emergency calls in our core service area, and we offer same-day appointments for everything else. When you call, the dispatcher gives you a real arrival window — and your tech texts you a live ETA.

How much does a spring replacement cost?+

Most spring replacements fall in a flat-rate range we'll quote you on the phone based on your door size and spring type. The price we quote is the price you pay — approved by you before any work begins. Don't forget coupon SPRING75.

Do you charge extra for nights, weekends, or holidays?+

No. Our 24/7 availability is real availability — the rate is the same at 2 PM on a Tuesday as it is at 11 PM on a Sunday.

Should I try to fix a broken spring myself?+

Please don't. Garage door springs are under extreme tension and cause thousands of serious injuries every year. It's the one repair every manufacturer says to leave to trained technicians with the right tools.

Do you work on my door brand?+

Yes — we repair and install all major brands including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Clopay, Amarr, Wayne Dalton, and Overhead Door, plus older and off-brand doors.

Is the work guaranteed?+

Every repair is backed by our workmanship guarantee, lifetime warranty on spring labor, and up to 10 years on parts. If anything we touched isn't right, we come back and fix it free.
